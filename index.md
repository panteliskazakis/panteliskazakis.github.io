I am an **Assistant Professor (Lecturer in the UK)** at the Adam Smith Business School, University of Glasgow. I received my Ph.D. from The Ohio State University.

My research interests centre on corporate finance, corporate taxation, banking, labour finance, and entrepreneurial finance. My work also spans innovation, regional and urban economics, economic inequality, and public finance.
